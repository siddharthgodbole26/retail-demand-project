## Retail Demand Forecasting & Inventory Replenishment Planner

### Part A — Problem Framing

**Business Objective**  
Reduce stockouts (lost sales + unhappy customers) while controlling inventory holding costs by improving demand forecasting and replenishment decisions at store–SKU level.

**North Star Metric (chosen & justified)**  
- **Service level / fill rate** improvement (and corresponding reduction in stockout rate), supported by **lost sales avoided (units and ₹)**.  
  - Higher service level directly reflects better on-shelf availability for customers.
  - Lost sales avoided translates service improvements into financial impact.

**Supporting KPIs (8–12)**  
- **Forecast accuracy**: MAPE / WAPE by category and overall.
- **Stockout rate**: % of days with `stockout_flag = 1` by store–SKU / category.
- **Fill rate**: demand fulfilled ÷ true demand (1 − stockout-censored share).
- **Inventory turns (proxy)**: annualized (or period) units sold ÷ average on-hand inventory.
- **Days of inventory on hand (DOH)**: `on_hand_units / avg_daily_demand`.
- **Overstock rate**: % of inventory positions with DOH above a category threshold (e.g., > 30/45/60 days).
- **Holding cost proxy**: inventory value × assumed holding cost rate.
- **Lost sales proxy**: `true_demand_units − units_sold` when stockouts occur, valued at price or margin.

**Scope Definition**  
- **Forecasting horizon**: next **28 days** (4 weeks) of daily demand.
- **Granularity**: **store–SKU** level (can be rolled up to category, region, or store).

**Stakeholder Questions (examples)**  
1. Where are we experiencing the highest stockout risk (stores/SKUs/regions) and what is the lost sales impact?
2. Which SKUs or categories are driving most of our lost sales and why (demand volatility, lead times, policy gaps)?
3. How accurate are our demand forecasts by category, and which method performs best?
4. Where are we overstocked (high DOH) and how much capital is tied up there?
5. What reorder points and safety stocks should we use per store–SKU to hit target service levels?
6. What is the projected 30‑day impact of adopting this replenishment policy on stockouts, lost sales, and inventory value?
7. What risks, assumptions, or data quality issues could limit the reliability of these recommendations?

### Project Overview & North Star

This project builds an end-to-end **retail demand forecasting and inventory replenishment planner** at store–SKU level.

- **North Star**: improve on-shelf availability while controlling working capital, measured primarily via **service level / fill rate** and **lost sales avoided**.
- Supporting goals:
  - **Forecast demand** at store–SKU level for the next 28 days.
  - **Identify stockout and overstock risk** based on projected demand vs inventory.
  - **Recommend reorder quantities** using a reorder-point + safety-stock policy.
  - **Estimate business impact** and surface results via an Excel dashboard.

### Forecasting Methods Used (Planned)

The analysis (in `analysis/`) will compare at least:

- **Baseline**: simple moving-average / seasonal-naive demand forecast using `true_demand_units`.
- **Model-based**: a lightweight regression or tree-based model that uses calendar features (weekday, weekend, promo, holiday) and possibly store/SKU attributes.

Forecast accuracy (MAPE/WAPE) will be evaluated by category and overall, and the better-performing approach will be used to drive replenishment simulations.

### Curated Outputs Generated

The ETL writes three main curated datasets into `data/`:

- **`fact_sales_store_sku_daily.csv`**  
  Daily store–SKU sales facts with:
  - `date`, `store_id`, `sku_id`
  - `units_sold`, `revenue`, `margin_proxy`
  - `promo_flag`, `holiday_flag`, `day_of_week`
  - `true_demand_units`, `stockout_censored_units`

- **`fact_inventory_store_sku_daily.csv`**  
  Daily store–SKU inventory facts with:
  - `date`, `store_id`, `sku_id`
  - `on_hand_units`
  - `stockout_flag`
  - `days_of_cover` (based on recent average demand)

- **`replenishment_inputs_store_sku.csv`**  
  Store–SKU level replenishment planning inputs with:
  - `avg_daily_demand`, `demand_std_dev`
  - `lead_time_days`
  - `service_level_target`, `safety_stock`, `reorder_point`
  - latest `on_hand_units`
  - `recommended_order_qty`
  - `category`, `shelf_life_days`, `moq_units`
